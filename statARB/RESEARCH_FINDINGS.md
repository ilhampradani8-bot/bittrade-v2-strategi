# RESEARCH FINDINGS: Statistical Arbitrage (Bot E)

## 1. Project Summary & History
The statARB project aimed to exploit mean-reverting properties (co-integration) of cryptocurrency pairs using a Rolling Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Z-score approach.

**Initial Implementation & Bugs Discovered:**
- **Fake Scanner Data:** Early scanner iterations used placeholder randomization instead of actual historical prices, leading to false positive test results.
- **Rolling Mean Overload:** Initial Z-score formulas were naive (`(ratio - mean)/std`) and did not respect the OLS beta relationship correctly. 
- **EV Gate Unit Error:** Expected Value calculations incorrectly mixed percentages with decimals (e.g. `0.20` meaning 20% vs 0.20%). 
- **Variable Beta Contamination:** Z-score exits were initially calculated using live beta values instead of locking the entry beta, causing false PnL calculations where the dynamic quantity implied by beta created non-existent gains.

Once all mathematical bugs were fixed and true walk-forward simulations were built, the empirical data revealed the strategy's flaws.

## 2. Validated Walk-Forward Methodology
For any future research, the following methodology **MUST** be used to ensure valid simulation (see `stage2_validation.py` for reference implementation):
- **Entry-Fixed Qty (Locked Beta):** Once a position is opened, `qty_a` and `qty_b` must be locked based on the beta at entry time. PnL is exclusively calculated using `(exit_price - entry_price) * locked_qty`.
- **Absolute Dollar Constraints:** PnL must be measured in absolute USDT (not Z-score proxies), clamped between Exchange `min_notional` limits (e.g., $5 to $20) and capital limits (e.g. $80 max for a $200 account).
- **Realistic Fee Hurdle:** Binance Futures VIP 0 fee is 0.05% per leg. Round-trip for a spread (2 legs, enter and exit) yields a **0.10% total fee burden** per gross execution value.
- **Sub-Period Walk-Forward:** Models must be trained and tested over sequential unseen periods (e.g., 3 sub-periods of 10 days each) to ensure edge robustness across regimes.

## 3. Results Summary

### Phase A: ETH/BTC Sweep (7 Timeframe Combinations)
Extensively tested ETH/BTC using timeframes ranging from 1-second ticks to 5-minute bars.
- **Result:** No timeframe produced an edge large enough to surpass the 0.10% fee hurdle. The most "promising" timeframes simply lost less money.

### Phase B: Manual 6 Altcoin Pick
Tested UNI/AAVE, PEPE/FLOKI, SOL/AVAX on 1-min data.
- **Result:** R2 degradation and volatility gaps showed that manual sector picks (e.g. "both are meme coins") do not guarantee co-integration. All failed the EV gate.

### Phase C: 528-Pair Systematic Screening
Automated pipeline testing every USDⓈ-M Futures pair against BTC and ETH.
- **Stage 1 (Fast Filter):** 1,050 combinations tested (15m data). Only **32 pairs** passed strict co-integration (`R² >= 0.85`) and Implied Edge (>0.15%).
- **Stage 2 (Deep Validation):** Top 5 candidates tested with full 1-minute walk-forward over 30 days.
  - `GUAUSDT / ETHUSDT`: Failed (2/3 periods negative)
  - `LITUSDT / ETHUSDT`: Failed (All periods negative)
  - `BANANAUSDT / ETHUSDT`: Failed (All periods negative)
  - `BANANAUSDT / BTCUSDT`: Failed (All periods negative)
  - `ALTUSDT / BTCUSDT`: Failed (All periods negative)
- **Result:** 0 pairs passed. 

*Raw data for all 528 pairs is archived in `statARB/research_data/screening_528pairs.csv`.*

## 4. Final Conclusion
**The Linear OLS Statistical Arbitrage strategy is NOT VIABLE at the Binance 0.10% fee structure.**
The gross edge generated by Z-score mean-reversion is consistently smaller than the fee hurdle. When observing pairs with high enough volatility to theoretically cover fees, the model signals extremely high frequency trades (100+ per week), causing cumulative fee drag to inevitably destroy all gross profit.

## 5. Future Research Directions (Suspended)
If this project is ever resumed, do not attempt to tune buffer or fee numbers. Instead, pivot the core mathematical approach:
- **Kalman Filters:** Replace Rolling OLS with Kalman filters for dynamic, non-lagging hedge ratios.
- **Wider Timeframes:** Move to Daily/Weekly bars where the gross edge (moves of 2-5%) vastly outscales the 0.10% fee, though trades will be very infrequent.
- **Non-Arbitrage Models:** Pivot away from market-neutral spread trading entirely, exploring directional volatility breakout models.
