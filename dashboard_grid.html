📖 Advanced Trading Engine Manual

A. System Indicators & Badges

  • Winrate Bot: The percentage of successful (profitable) trades out of all executed sells.
  • CPU & RAM: Real-time VPS resource monitoring. Ensures the bot is not lagging.
  • Whale Alert: Detects massive institutional volume spikes. DETECTED! 🐋 means smart money is actively buying.

B. Process Pipeline (LED Monitors)

The 5-stage pipeline represents the real-time event loop of the trading engine:

  • WebSocket: Streams live ticks from Binance (1-second intervals).
  • AI Pattern: Detects candlestick patterns and basic formations.
  • Conclude: The strategy brain. Analyzes regimes and calculates MTFA parameters.
  • Validate: The safety layer. Blocks bad trades and enforces risk management (e.g., stopping trades during excessive spread).
  • Executor: Connects to the Exchange API to place the actual Buy/Sell orders.

C. Multi-Timeframe Analysis (MTFA)

The core trading strategy relies on 3 simultaneous timeframes to confirm a perfect entry point.

1. The 1H Layer (Macro Trend)

Analyzes the general market sentiment using Bollinger Bands.

  • BULLISH: Price is hugging the upper band. Uptrend confirmed.
  • SIDEWAYS: Ranging market. Wait for breakout.
  • BEARISH: Downtrend. Highly risky to buy.

2. The 15M Layer (Local Momentum)

Hunts for market pullbacks or discount zones within the trend.

  • OVERSOLD: Price is too cheap locally. High probability bounce.
  • OVERBOUGHT: Price is too expensive locally. Wait for correction.

3. The 1M Layer (Whale Detector Trigger)

The sniper trigger looking for volume anomalies and Taker Buy Ratios.

  • PUMP DETECTED: Volume spiked 300%+ with >75% Taker Buys. EXECUTE.
  • DUMP DETECTED: Massive volume but dominated by Taker Sells. ABORT.
Perfect Sniper Setup: 1H [BULLISH] + 15M [OVERSOLD] + 1M [PUMP DETECTED]
